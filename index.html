How to play

  1. Everyone gets a secret character. You cannot see your own.
  2. Ask a yes/no question for everyone to answer.
  3. Use the answers to narrow down your character.
  4. Guess the character for +1 point, the title for +2 points, or both for +3 total.
  5. In multiplayer, share the six-character room code with your friends.

NEITHER means the question does not clearly apply or the answer is unknown.
